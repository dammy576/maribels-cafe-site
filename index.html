Our Story

Maribel opened this little shop in her grandmother's old storefront because she believed a neighborhood needs a place to gather — no rush, no pretense, just good coffee and familiar faces. Every cup is poured by someone who knows your name (or is about to). We roast small batches, bake fresh every morning, and keep the porch light on for regulars and newcomers alike.
